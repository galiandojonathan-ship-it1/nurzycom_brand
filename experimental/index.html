05 — FAQ

Straight answers

Will you guarantee a score?

No. After a diagnostic you get a realistic range and a plan.

Can one course prepare for both exams?

For reading, vocabulary and grammar, largely yes. Speaking, Listening and essays are IELTS-only and get their own lessons. The combined course gives each SAT skill less time than the full SAT course does.

What level do I need?

The diagnostic decides. [FACT NEEDED] minimum level for the combined course.

Are the practice questions official?

No. Every question in the demo is original, written for NURZYCOM. Official questions are never copied.
