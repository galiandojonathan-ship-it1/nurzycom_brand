03 — How lessons work

A plan, step by step.

  1. I

    Diagnose

    An official Bluebook practice test. Every mistake goes into an error log, sorted by question type.

  2. II

    Target

    Lessons on the 2–3 question types that lose the most points. A timed module every week.

  3. III

    Prove

    A full practice test every 2–3 weeks, and a short score update to parents.

No guarantees. After the diagnostic you get a realistic range for your score, and a plan to reach it.
